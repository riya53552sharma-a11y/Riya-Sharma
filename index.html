Undergraduate Researcher — Municipal Solid Waste & Resource Recovery

National College of Engineering · 2022–2023

Resource and Energy Recovery from Municipal Solid Waste – Lalitpur Metropolitan City

The research characterized municipal solid waste generated in Lalitpur Metropolitan City Ward 14 and evaluated the feasibility of material and energy recovery based on its physical and chemical characteristics.

81.55% Organic waste
53.24% Moisture content
1.877 kg Waste/day/household
4.133 MJ/kg Organic heat content
  • Characterized municipal solid waste through household surveys, waste sampling, field measurements, laboratory analysis and interviews.
  • Determined waste generation rate, composition, density, moisture content, calorific value, pH and NPK characteristics.
  • Evaluated the feasibility of material and energy recovery from the municipal waste stream.
  • Assessed recycling, composting and energy-recovery pathways based on waste characteristics.
  • Evaluated the suitability of organic waste for composting and organic fertilizer production.
  • Conducted quantitative analysis of recoverable materials, including plastics and paper, and assessed their potential for recycling and reuse.

Selected Academic Projects

Selected engineering, laboratory and analytical projects.

RCC Beam & Concrete Laboratory Investigation

  • Prepared M20 concrete using a 1:1.5:3 nominal mix with a 0.50 water–cement ratio.
  • Fabricated concrete cube specimens and a reinforced concrete beam using Fe415 reinforcement.
  • Conducted slump cone testing to evaluate the workability of the prepared concrete mix.
  • Performed Schmidt rebound hammer and Universal Testing Machine (UTM) tests on concrete cubes to evaluate compressive strength.
  • Obtained a compressive strength of 28.7 MPa using UTM and approximately 30 MPa using the rebound hammer.
  • Tested the reinforced concrete beam to evaluate moment/flexural resistance and failure behavior, observing shear cracking.
  • Performed theoretical calculations of reinforcement, neutral axis and moment of resistance.
  • Compared analytical calculations with experimental results.

Hydropower Potential Assessment Using Flow Duration and Power Duration Curves

  • Developed flow duration curves from streamflow records.
  • Generated power duration curves to evaluate hydropower generation potential.
  • Performed hydrological data analysis using Microsoft Excel.
  • Assessed river discharge variability for hydropower planning.

Seismic Performance Evaluation of a 3-Story Reinforced Concrete Frame Using ETABS

  • Developed a 3D ETABS model of an RC moment-resisting frame.
  • Applied gravity and lateral seismic loads based on code provisions.
  • Performed modal analysis and response spectrum analysis.
  • Evaluated interstory drift, base shear and structural response parameters.
