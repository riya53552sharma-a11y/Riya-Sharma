About

Engineering with a Research Perspective

I am a Civil Engineering graduate from National College of Engineering, affiliated with Tribhuvan University, Nepal, with professional experience in construction engineering, structural modeling, site supervision, engineering documentation, and reinforced concrete structures.

During my undergraduate studies, I worked on research related to municipal solid waste characterization and resource recovery. This experience gave me practical exposure to field investigation, waste sampling, laboratory analysis, data interpretation and technical research.

My current research interests are focused on sustainable and low-carbon construction materials, circular economy approaches, bio-based and waste-derived materials, and the structural performance of innovative materials.

I am interested in pursuing graduate research opportunities where civil engineering can contribute to more sustainable, resource- efficient and resilient infrastructure.

Featured Research

Municipal Solid Waste Characterization & Resource Recovery Potential

Undergraduate Research Project

My undergraduate research investigated municipal solid waste in Lalitpur Metropolitan City Ward 14 and explored opportunities for composting, recycling, material recovery and energy recovery.

The study examined household waste generation, physical and chemical characteristics, moisture content, calorific value, nutrient composition and potential resource recovery pathways.
